Privacy policy — Atarivo Factory

Last updated 2026-11-09.

"Atarivo Factory" is an internal publishing tool operated by Atarivo for Atarivo's own accounts. It is not offered to the public, has no users other than Atarivo, and collects no personal information from anyone.

What the tool does

The tool prepares posts and pins that describe Atarivo's own products (digital planners, calendars, puzzle books and similar, sold on Etsy and Amazon) and Atarivo's own Apify Actors. On Pinterest it acts as a Pin scheduler for Atarivo's own business account: it composes candidate pins, the account owner chooses each pin by hand, and the tool publishes the chosen pins to the account's own boards.

What is stored

  • Records of the tool's own actions: the identifier a platform returns for a pin or post the tool created, the board it was placed on, the product link it carries, and the times it was chosen and published.
  • Aggregate statistics about Atarivo's own account (for example impressions and outbound clicks over the trailing 30 days), read from the platform's analytics API each time they are needed and kept only as the latest value.

Nothing about any other person or account is collected or stored. The tool does not read other users' pins, boards, profiles or messages, does not follow, comment, message or save on anyone's behalf, and does not store content fetched back from any platform. It uses only the API permissions needed to read and write Atarivo's own boards and pins and to read Atarivo's own account analytics.

Cookies and tracking

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. GitHub Pages, which hosts it, may log requests according to GitHub's privacy statement. Links in pins and posts go straight to the product page on Etsy, Amazon or Apify, which have their own privacy policies.

Access tokens

Credentials the tool uses to act on Atarivo's own accounts are stored encrypted on Atarivo's own systems, scoped to the minimum permissions required, and are never shared.

Contact

Questions about this policy: open an issue at github.com/atarivo/apify-actor-examples/issues.
